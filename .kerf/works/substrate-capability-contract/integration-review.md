# Integration Review — Declared Substrate Capability Contract

## Round 1

BLOCK. The design treated unsettled remote behavior as a public selected-mode
promise. It left the tmux/Claude paste callers undecided. It also described
post-spawn pane capabilities as pre-dispatch requirements.

## Round 2

BLOCK. The draft and table distinguished construction and operation checks, but
the lifecycle design and daemon traceability still used the old selected-mode
wording.

## Round 3

APPROVE. The design, draft, changelog, and integration record now distinguish
construction requirements from operation requirements. Remote helpers remain
internal operation checks. The paste caller and harness matrix preserves the
`InputPort` and AIS acceptance boundary. The Step 12 serialization and the
run-session removal closure remain intact.
