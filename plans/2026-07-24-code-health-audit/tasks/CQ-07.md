## Required work

1. Add controlled cuts after admission, reservation, claim, and before terminal
   persistence where current scenarios lack proof.
2. Assert durable queue, Bead, Run, receipt/status, and available observation
   facts after restart. `queue_group_completed` may be absent and is never the
   recovery oracle.
3. Use deterministic barriers/channels, never timing sleeps as the oracle.
4. Count launch/claim attempts and require one runnable owner.

## Acceptance

- A supported item is either executed once or classified safely after restart.
- No cut yields duplicate launch, leaked claim, or silent pending/dispatched
  limbo.
- The test runs without the primary daemon or real paid model.
- Existing happy-path behavior remains green.
- Suppressing the final observation still terminates through exact queue-ID
  live/receipt status; restart never synthesizes that event and same-name reuse
  cannot satisfy the old waiter.

changed helpers, review, and `make check-fast`.
