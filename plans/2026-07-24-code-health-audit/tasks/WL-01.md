## Scenario matrix

Every row must record `preconditions`, an ordered `required_trace`,
`forbidden_effects`, classification (`invariant`, `observed_debt`, or `gap`),
and a named test proof.

- `queue_success`: queue select → durable reservation → claim → register →
  executor start/terminal → queue terminal → unregister; no `br ready`.
- `br_ready_success`: ready → show-open → claim → register → executor
  start/terminal → ledger close/reopen → unregister; no queue mutation.
- `loaded_queue_suppresses_br_ready`: a loaded but non-contributing queue
  waits/wakes without calling `Ready`.
- `idle_submit_wake`: a blocked idle loop wakes from the queue-store wake
  channel and re-evaluates without wall-clock sleep.
- `handler_pause_queue`: selection or held-event observation may occur, but no
  dispatch stamp, claim, registration, or spawn.
- `operator_pause_br_ready`: no ready poll, claim, registration, or spawn.
- `disk_low_gate` and `capacity_gate`: maintenance/gate occurs before source
  selection; no source, claim, or spawn.
- `reservation_persist_failure`: record whether claim occurs without a durable
  reservation, classify it `observed_debt`, and do not promote the current
  continuation or refusal to a normative invariant.
- `queue_claim_failure`: durable reservation precedes claim; failure
  reverts/persists pending, records failure, and never registers or spawns.
- `spawn_completion`: register precedes executor start; queue/ledger terminal
  effects precede unregister.
- `shutdown_inflight`: stop new dispatch, await or timeout the run, never record
  a false queue failure on cancellation, cancel the queue, then return.
- `restart_spawn_gate`: no source effect before `spawnSubstrateReadyCh` closes;
  dispatch may begin afterward.
- `restart_live_session`: adoption monitors the existing session; disappearance
  causes ledger reopen, dispatched→pending persistence/wake, then run-record
  removal.
