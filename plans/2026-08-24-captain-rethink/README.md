**The rule that governs the plan: default to delete.** A line survives only when someone can
name the failure it prevents and that failure is not already prevented by a shorter line
inside the four target pages.

**The order: cut first, refine what survives.** Nobody should have to hold fifty kilobytes in
their head to decide what the captain is for. That conversation happens in Step 3, over what
is left.

**The number: ~48.5 K tokens of files at boot, and that is a floor.** 57 % of it is one
section of one shell script's output. Read `01-inventory.md` before deciding what to work on.
